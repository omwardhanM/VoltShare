With rapid residential solar adoption under schemes like PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana across urban centers in Gujarat (e.g., Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Anand), a critical socio-technical bottleneck has emerged: the midday solar duck-curve paradox. Prosumers (residential solar producers) generate excess power between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM when household baseload is minimal. Concurrently, apartment dwellers and non-solar consumers purchase expensive coal-heavy electricity from utility grids at peak retail tariffs (₹6.50 – ₹7.50/kWh), while prosumers receive a meager feed-in compensation (~₹2.25/kWh) for grid export.

By establishing a local peer-to-peer (P2P) energy marketplace, prosumers sell surplus solar electricity to neighbors at ₹4.20 – ₹5.00/kWh, unlocking a win-win economic model: prosumers double their feed-in earnings, while consumers save 25–35% on electricity bills.

Problem Statement & CPS Engineering Context

1. The Urban Renewable Energy Dilemma

In urban Gujarat, residential rooftop solar penetration has escalated significantly. However, electricity distribution networks operate on centralized unidirectional legacy architectures. This creates two distinct pain points:

  • Prosumer Curtailment & Economic Dissatisfaction: Prosumers who invested in 3 kW to 6 kW rooftop arrays generate 14–25 kWh daily. Midday self-consumption rarely exceeds 20–30% of instantaneous generation. The remaining power flows to the utility DISCOM (e.g., MGVCL) at a low gross/net metering credit rate of ~₹2.25/kWh, lengthening capital payback periods to 7–9 years.
  • Consumer Lock-Out in Multi-Story Apartments: High-density urban populations reside in apartment complexes where individual rooftop ownership is legally and physically unfeasible. These consumers remain tethered to thermal coal-generated grid power at tier tariffs exceeding ₹7.10/kWh.

Core Implementation & Key Algorithms

1. P2P Dynamic Matching & Escrow Settlement Engine

The core trading mechanism guarantees transactional safety, buyer fund verification, and instantaneous carbon ledger calculation:

ALGORITHM: Atomic P2P Energy Settlement
1. INPUT: listing_id, buyer_id, requested_kwh
2. RETRIEVE listing & buyer FROM database WHERE status IN ('active', 'partially_sold')
3. IF (requested_kwh > listing.available_kwh) RETURN Error("Exceeds pool")
4. SET total_cost = ROUND(requested_kwh × listing.price_per_kwh, 2)
5. IF (buyer.wallet_balance < total_cost) RETURN Error("Insufficient escrow")
6. COMPUTE co2_saved = ROUND(requested_kwh × 0.82, 2) // Central Electricity Auth Benchmark
7. COMPUTE grid_savings = MAX(0, ROUND((requested_kwh × 7.10) - total_cost, 2))
8. BEGIN TRANSACTION:
    UPDATE energy_listings SET available_kwh = available_kwh - requested_kwh
    UPDATE users SET wallet_balance = wallet_balance - total_cost WHERE id = buyer_id
    UPDATE users SET wallet_balance = wallet_balance + total_cost WHERE id = listing.seller_id
    INSERT INTO p2p_trades (listing_id, buyer_id, seller_id, kwh_traded, total_cost, co2_saved, grid_savings)
9. COMMIT TRANSACTION; RETURN TradeReceipt & DigitalProof
